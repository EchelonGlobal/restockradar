Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

RestockRadar is a pre-launch SMS restock-alert service. This policy explains what we collect when you join the founding list and how we use it. Plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • Name, email, and phone number: whatever you enter on our signup form.
  • Category interest: which product category you pick (sneakers, tech, collectibles, tickets, other).
  • SMS opt-in choice: whether you checked the box to receive text alerts.

How we use it

  • To hold your founding-list spot and your $1.99/mo locked rate.
  • To email and text you when RestockRadar launches.
  • To send restock alert texts: only if you opted in by checking the SMS box.

Our signup form is processed by FormSubmit, which emails your submission to us. To run the service we share your information only with the providers that deliver it: FormSubmit (form delivery), Twilio (sending text messages), and Stripe (payments, when billing goes live). They may only use it to provide their service to us. We do not sell your information, and we never share your phone number with anyone for their own marketing.

Your privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we have about you, to delete it, to correct it, and to tell us not to sell or share it. We do not sell your information. You also have the right not to be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, email fcarrasco@echelonls.com. We will respond within 45 days. Someone you authorize can make the request for you; we will need proof they can act on your behalf.

How long we keep your information

We keep your signup information while your waitlist spot or account is active. If you cancel or ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days. One exception: we keep your SMS opt-in consent record as long as the law requires, because carriers and regulators can ask for proof you agreed to receive texts.

Link tracking

Buy links in our alerts may record that you clicked and when, so we can tell which alerts are working. Our pages themselves do not set tracking cookies.

Security

We limit access to your information to what is needed to run the service, and our providers are required to protect it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

RestockRadar is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn we have it, we delete it.

Text messages

If you opt in, you agree to receive recurring automated restock alert texts from RestockRadar at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. We never send texts between 9pm and 8am your time. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts immediately.
  • Email fcarrasco@echelonls.com to ask what we have on file or to delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: fcarrasco@echelonls.com.
